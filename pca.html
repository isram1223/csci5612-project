PCA

Added in Project Part 2.

Overview

Principal Component Analysis (PCA) is an unsupervised dimensionality reduction technique that mathematically transforms a high-dimensional dataset into a smaller set of uncorrelated variables (principal components) while preserving as much variance as possible. Working with 35 distinct socioeconomic and health indicators creates challenges for both visualization and analysis. PCA addresses this by calculating eigenvectors (the directions of maximum variance) and projecting the complex county-level dataset onto a simplified coordinate space, allowing us to uncover the primary drivers of community distress.
